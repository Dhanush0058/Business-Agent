Signature Program

CKO Kickboxing

Our main, high-energy class is designed to burn up to 1,200 calories per session. Punch, kick, sweat, and melt stress away on your own heavy bag.

What To Expect

The 45-Minute Workout Flow

01

Warm Up

10 minutes of dynamic movement, calisthenics, and light stretching to elevate your heart rate and prepare joints for bag work.

02

Bag Work

25 minutes of combinations—jabs, crosses, hooks, kicks, and knees. Work at your own pace and power level under instructor guidance.

03

Core Blast

7 minutes of intensive abdominal work targeting obliques, upper, and lower core using planks, crunches, and conditioning.

04

Cool Down

3 minutes of slow breathing and deep static stretching to aid recovery, reduce muscle tension, and finish class feeling accomplished.
