Programs & Circuits

Group Fitness

High-energy HIIT, functional circuits, strength intervals, and community coaching. Reach your peak physical condition, surrounded by support.

Stronger Together, No One Left Behind

Our group classes are built around community motivation. We combine cardio boxing drills with core conditioning, resistance bands, and bodyweight training to maximize calorie burn and endurance.

High Intensity Interval Training (HIIT)

Short bursts of explosive effort followed by brief recovery rounds keep metabolism elevated for up to 36 hours post-workout.

Heart Rate Friendly & Self-Paced

Exercises are easily modified for beginner, intermediate, or advanced practitioners. Go as heavy or light as your body feels.
